Chapter — About

A builder first, a creative always.

I'm a Software Engineering with Multimedia student at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology, working across full-stack development, database systems, UI/UX design, and brand storytelling. My background sits at an unusual intersection: I write the backend that runs a system, then design the interface it ships in, then shoot the campaign that promotes it.

Outside the editor, I model professionally and perform as a dancer — disciplines that taught me presence, direction, and how to represent a brand on stage the same way I represent a product on screen.
